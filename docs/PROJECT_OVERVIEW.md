# InterviewIQ

### Practice the real interview. Get interview-ready. Get hired.

An AI interviewer that knows your background and knows how your target company interviews. It
takes you through that company's full interview process, round by round, until you're ready for the
real thing.

> *Each section below is one slide.*

---

## The problem

**Candidates**

- Real mock interviews are **scarce and expensive**. Seniors are busy, mentors are hard to find, and
  coaching costs money.
- The practice they do get is **generic and inconsistent**. It doesn't reflect how *their* target
  company interviews.
- Coding-practice sites are **solo**. Solving problems alone is not the same as explaining your
  thinking to an interviewer under time pressure.
- So most people walk into the real interview having **never rehearsed it**, and they learn in the
  one that counts.

**Colleges**

- Placement cells are judged on outcomes, but have **no scalable way** to give hundreds of students
  real interview practice.

**Companies**

- Hiring is **slow and costly**. Screening rounds filter out most applicants, and recruiters still
  can't easily find candidates who are genuinely ready.

## Your interview roadmap

**Pick a company and a role, and we show you how its interview process works.**

- Each role has its own process: **how many rounds, what each round covers** (coding, system design,
  behavioural, hiring manager, and so on), **how hard it is, and what interviewers look for.**
- It appears on **your personal dashboard** for that role. The dashboard starts almost empty and
  fills in as you schedule and clear rounds.
- You work through it **one round at a time**, at your own pace.
- **You can customise it:** add, remove or change rounds to focus on what you need.

> **Only the official process counts.** If you edit the suggested process, those interviews become
> **standalone practice**. They still get full analysis, but they don't count towards your
> readiness for that role.

**We research how companies really interview, continuously, so the practice gets as close to the

- A research engine runs **in the background, all the time**. It gathers real interview experiences
  that candidates have shared across the internet.
- It focuses on the **right context**: the right country, the right level (fresher vs experienced),

- It **keeps updating** as new experiences appear, so the process stays current.

This body of knowledge grows with every company and every interview. It is hard to copy.

## Inside the interview

**It should feel like a real remote interview, not a chatbot.**

- **Built on your data.** The interviewer uses what it knows about you, including your projects,
  experience and coding history, to personalise its questions.
- **A real conversation.**
  - It greets you, chats briefly about your background, and explains the format.
  - It listens, acknowledges your answer, and asks one question at a time.
- **It adapts as you go:**
  - **Stuck?** It gives a hint, from gentle to direct.
  - **Doing well?** It pushes with harder follow-ups.
  - **Struggling?** It eases off.
  - **Off track?** It steers you back.
- **Hands-on.** A built-in code editor and whiteboard. The interviewer reads your code and diagrams
  as you work.
- **Real-time analysis** of many signals throughout: correctness, approach, depth, communication,
  clarity, hesitation, filler words, and how you recover after a hint.
- **Proctored.** Leaving full-screen or switching tabs pauses the interview until you return.

## After every interview

**A detailed debrief, not just a number.**

- **Overall score** for the round.
- **Question by question:** what you said, and **what a strong answer would have looked like.**
- **What to learn next:** the topics and skills to work on before your next attempt.
- **Strengths and weak spots** across the signals analysed during the interview.

**Then:**

- ✅ **Above the round's pass mark:** the next round unlocks and your readiness level goes up.
- 🔁 **Below it:** retake the round after **1–2 weeks**, enough time to work on the feedback.
- 🎯 **All rounds cleared:** you're **interview-ready** for that role, and your readiness report is
  ready to share.

## Readiness, scores and leaderboards

  | Level | What it means |
  |---|---|
  | Getting started | Interview process set up, no rounds cleared yet |
  | Progressing | Some rounds cleared |
  | Nearly ready | One round left |

- 📄 **Readiness report.** Unlocked when you're interview-ready. A shareable summary of the rounds,
  scores, strengths and practice history behind it.
- 🎯 **Interview score.** How you perform, based on every interview you've taken.
- 🏆 **Global leaderboards** on the interview score, with more ways to compete over time.

## Why "interview-ready" means something

**Interview-ready is a signal colleges and companies can trust, not a participation trophy.**

- **The company's real process.** Only the official, unedited process counts.
- **Every round has a pass mark.** You can't skip ahead.
- **Retakes need a cooldown** of 1–2 weeks, so you can't brute-force it.
- **Proctored interviews**: full-screen, no tab switching.
- **Personal questions.** They are drawn from your own background, so the answers can't be
  memorised from a list.

- **Honest wording.** It is always presented as readiness from mock interviews, never as a
  certification or an endorsement by the company.

This is what makes the final step possible: companies hiring directly from candidates who are
interview-ready for their process, with the readiness report as evidence.
