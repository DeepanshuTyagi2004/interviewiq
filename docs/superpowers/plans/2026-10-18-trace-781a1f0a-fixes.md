## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-18)

1. **Refusal ceiling of 3 for everyone.** After 3 refusals on one problem, "resist" is no longer an allowed action. The 4th ask gets `change` or `end_coding`, as the model or the prior chooses. Everything else in the 2026-10-18 model-decides rule stays.
2. **"Explain the problem" goes on the screen only.** The interviewer writes a plain-language explanation with `add_section` (`kind=explanation`). Aloud, it says one short line pointing to it. It never explains aloud. On a second ask for the same problem, it asks which part is unclear.
3. **Endpointing: merge fragments; the tuning stays.** The end-of-turn threshold stays at 0.30 and the minimum delay at 0.5 s. A candidate fragment whose reply was cut before any audio is answered together with its continuation. A fragment ending on a dangling word counts as unfinished.
4. **Deep dive: one area at a time, in outline order.** The focus is the first promised area in `promises_of(blueprint)` order. Only that area's questions are shown. The focus moves on after 2 questions on the area or a "don't know". The director sees the focus. There is no going back.
